Alba Cove Trading FZCO is an international trading and business development company headquartered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

Established in the UAE, one of the world’s leading commercial and logistics hubs, we focus on global commodity trading, industrial raw materials, metals and international business facilitation. Our integrated model combines trading, strategic investments, business development and project coordination across international markets.

We were founded to connect businesses, investors and commercial opportunities across industries. Rather than operating as a traditional trading company, Alba Cove serves as a strategic business platform, bringing together commercial expertise, professional coordination and access to international markets, with reliability, transparency, compliance and efficient execution.
